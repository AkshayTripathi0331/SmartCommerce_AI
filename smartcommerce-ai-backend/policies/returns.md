# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return Window

You have **30 days** from the date of delivery to return most items for a full refund.

## Eligibility

Items are eligible for return if they are:
- Unused and in original condition
- In original packaging with all tags attached
- Accompanied by proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt)

### Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- Personalized or custom-made products
- Perishable goods
- Downloadable software or digital products
- Intimate apparel and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Items marked as "Final Sale"

## How to Start a Return

1. **Log into your account** and go to Order History
2. **Select the item(s)** you wish to return
3. **Choose your reason** for the return
4. **Print the prepaid shipping label** (free returns on all orders)
5. **Drop off the package** at any authorized shipping location

## Refund Process

- **Processing Time**: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return
- **Refund Method**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- **Shipping Costs**: Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different size or color:
1. Return the original item
2. Place a new order for the desired item

## Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item:
1. Contact us within **48 hours** of delivery
2. Include photos of the damage
3. We will provide a prepaid return label and send a replacement immediately

## Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund after 10 business days:
1. Check your bank account or credit card statement
2. Contact your bank (processing times vary)
3. If still not received, contact our support team

For return questions, email returns@smartcommerce.ai or call 1-800-SHOP-NOW.
