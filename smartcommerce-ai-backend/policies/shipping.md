# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Options

We offer the following shipping options for your convenience:

### Standard Shipping
- **Delivery Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Cost**: Free for orders over $50, otherwise $5.99
- **Tracking**: Full tracking available via email

### Express Shipping
- **Delivery Time**: 2-3 business days
- **Cost**: $12.99
- **Tracking**: Real-time tracking with SMS updates

### Next-Day Delivery
- **Delivery Time**: 1 business day (order by 2 PM local time)
- **Cost**: $24.99
- **Availability**: Select metro areas only

## International Shipping

We ship to over 50 countries worldwide.

- **Delivery Time**: 7-21 business days depending on destination
- **Cost**: Calculated at checkout based on weight and destination
- **Customs**: Customer is responsible for any customs duties or import taxes

## Order Processing

- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days
- You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once shipped
- Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding holidays

## Shipping Restrictions

- We cannot ship to P.O. boxes for express or next-day delivery
- Some items may have shipping restrictions due to size or regulations
- Hazardous materials cannot be shipped internationally

## Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged:
1. Contact our customer support within 48 hours
2. Provide your order number and photos of damage (if applicable)
3. We will arrange a replacement or refund
